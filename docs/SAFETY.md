# Safety Notes / 安全说明

ACE-Omega is a research visualization tool. It is not an authority on academic quality, scientific truth or research value.

## Risks

- Citation counts may be incomplete or biased.
- AI interpretation may hallucinate.
- Uploaded files may contain private or copyrighted information.
- API-based AI backends may send data to external services.
- Streamlit apps should not be exposed to public networks without authentication.

## Recommendations

- Verify important findings manually.
- Use local Ollama or GGUF models for sensitive documents.
- Keep `config.yaml`, `.env`, models and private datasets out of Git.
- Treat generated visualizations as exploratory maps, not final judgments.

中文：ACE-Omega 是探索工具，不是终审裁判。它可以帮助你看见结构，但不能替你证明结论。
